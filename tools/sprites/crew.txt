# The Blast Crew redesign (2026-11): the hero, the eight genre-tier enemies
# and their bosses, and the power-up tokens. Read after mockup.txt and
# extra.txt, so anything named here replaces the older drawing.
#
# Hero: a demolition kid in a hard hat with goggles pushed up and an orange
# bandana. The hat and overalls carry the player colour (players 2-4 are
# recolours of player 1), the bandana stays orange on everyone, and cosmetic
# hats sit over the hard hat. Deliberately unlike the genre's round white
# helmet with an antenna.
#
# Enemies keep the classic tier ladder (speed, smarts, points) but are our own
# creatures: Mallow, Snapclam, Windup, Zipbug, Gnawbit, Dustmoth, the Ringer
# (time-up hunter), King Mallow and the Clockwork Overlord. Sprite names stay
# as before so the rules and renderer keys don't change.
#
# Power-ups: round tokens coloured by family, with a bold white glyph and a
# dark outline: red = grow stronger (bomb, fire, speed), blue = new move
# (passes, kick, remote), purple = one-shot or team item, gold = 1UP. Each
# glyph is also exported alone as icon-<name> for the HUD.
# The power-up section is generated by crew_tokens.py.
